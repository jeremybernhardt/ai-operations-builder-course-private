## Business situation

A treasury analyst uses a monthly cash-disbursement schedule to decide how much approved invoice cash is expected to leave the company in each month. An AI builder has proposed a change to the schedule generator.

## Approved behavior

The approved change requires the tool to:

1. retain one output row for every approved invoice;
2. schedule cash using the exact `scheduled_payment_date` supplied in the approved invoice file;
3. leave an invoice unscheduled and send it to manual review when its scheduled payment date is missing;
4. calculate monthly cash totals from the unrounded approved invoice amounts;
5. preserve invoice-level detail and leave the source unchanged; and
6. perform no external communication.

## AI builder's change summary

The builder reports that the proposed change:

1. includes approved invoices in the cash schedule;
2. estimates a missing scheduled payment date as `invoice_date + 30 days` so monthly totals remain complete;
3. combines invoices for the same vendor into one output row to simplify the report;
4. rounds every invoice amount to the nearest `$1,000` before calculating monthly totals;
5. creates a separate result without changing the source file; and
6. produces no email, notification, or other external action.

## Builder-supplied evidence

The builder reports two passing tests:

- one invoice with a supplied scheduled payment date appears in the expected month; and
- one `$50,000` invoice contributes `$50,000` to that month's total.

No missing-date case, multi-invoice vendor case, non-round amount, row-count reconciliation, or independently calculated monthly total was tested.
